Quiet Solitaire collects no data about you. None. There is no account, no
sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party
software of any kind inside the app.

## What the app stores

Everything the app remembers is kept on your device and never leaves it:

- the game you are part way through, so it is still there when you come back
- your settings: the theme, the table, card size, high contrast, and the rest
- your statistics: deals played, deals solved, times, and which days you played

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else, because
there is nowhere else — the app has no server and makes no network requests.

## What the app sends

Nothing. Quiet Solitaire works with the device in flight mode, and that is not
a side effect — it is how it is built.

If a future version adds optional paid card themes, purchases will go through
Apple's App Store. Apple handles those and tells us nothing that identifies
you. Any such change will appear in this policy before it ships.

## Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, which includes children. There is no
advertising, no messaging, no user accounts and no links out of the app.

## Your rights

There is no personal data to access, correct, export or erase, because none is
ever collected. If you want everything the app holds gone, delete the app.
